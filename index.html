Current Research

Three threads I am working on right now: my thesis, my lab project and my own research direction.

M.Sc. thesis · in progress

VLA-Distillation: robots that keep their words at edge scale

My thesis argues that a compact vision–language–action model can speak and act coherently at edge scale when it is distilled from a large language-native planner. The argument is about where the representation boundary sits, not about scale.

  1. TeacherA fine-tuned mid-sized VLM outputs actions as JSON, keeping it close to human-readable language so it preserves its language ability while giving accurate actions.
  2. EncodeThe JSON action blocks are encoded into compact action tokens.
  3. StudentA custom edge-scale architecture is distilled from the teacher, so one model can both speak and act on local hardware.

Lab project · HUR Group

Upper-Limb Rehabilitation Chatbot

I manage the edge deployment of an LLM-centred system for patient interface control, inquiry, monitoring and grounded medical consultation on an upper-limb rehabilitation robot. I designed and built its fully local retrieval-augmented generation assistant: a versioned ingestion pipeline, hybrid dense and full-text retrieval fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, and streaming generation from MedGemma 1.5 4B, chosen after comparing six open-weight generators for its small footprint and medical-domain pretraining. I am now integrating an audio interface and own the MLOps and service integration across the system.

Qdrant · MongoDB · Ollama · FastAPI · MedGemma
